# Design System — Confer

<!-- impeccable:design-schema 1 -->

## Visual World: Obsidian Precision & Deep Zinc

Confer's visual language is engineered for high-focus, distraction-free video communication with high aesthetic polish.

### Color Palette

- **Canvas Base**: Obsidian (`#0B0C0E` / `rgb(11, 12, 14)`)
- **Surface Elevation 1**: Deep Zinc (`#121417` / `rgb(18, 20, 23)`)
- **Surface Elevation 2**: Slate Charcoal (`#1A1D21` / `rgb(26, 29, 33)`)
- **Surface Elevation 3 / Hover**: Elevated Steel (`#262A30` / `rgb(38, 42, 48)`)
- **Border Subtle**: `#22262C` / `rgb(34, 38, 44)`
- **Border Active / Focus**: Sky Accent (`#0284C7` / `#38BDF8`)
- **Active Speaker Indicator**: Emerald Pulse (`#10B981` / `#34D399`)
- **Destructive / Leave Action**: Crimson Rose (`#E11D48` / `#F43F5E`)
- **Warning / Alert**: Amber Flame (`#F59E0B` / `#FBBF24`)
- **Text Primary**: Crisp White (`#F8FAFC` / `rgb(248, 250, 252)`)
- **Text Secondary**: Muted Pewter (`#94A3B8` / `rgb(148, 163, 184)`)
- **Text Dim**: Dark Slate (`#64748B` / `rgb(100, 116, 139)`)

### Typography & Spacing

- **Scale**: Display (`28.0` - `32.0`), Heading (`16.0` - `20.0`), Body (`13.0` - `14.0`), Caption / Meta (`11.0` - `12.0`).
- **Corner Radii**: Buttons & Chips (`6.0` - `8.0`), Cards & Video Tiles (`10.0` - `12.0`), Bottom Floating Dock (`16.0`).
- **Grid Gutters**: Video grid gap `8.0px`, Card internal padding `16.0px` - `24.0px`.

### Motion & Micro-Interactions

- **Active Speaker Halo**: Smooth stroke width and color interpolation for participants speaking.
- **Emoji Reactions**: Upward floating particles with ease-out opacity decay.
- **Video Transitions**: Seamless instant cut between live hardware webcam texture and glassmorphic initial avatar card.
